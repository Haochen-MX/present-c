1. Present Continuous nima?

Present Continuous (yoki Present Progressive) — gap aytilayotgan paytda yoki shu davr atrofida davom etayotgan harakatni bildiradi. Harakat hali tugamagan, jarayon ketmoqda.

O'zbek tilida u odatda -yapman, -yapsan, -yapti, -moqda qo'shimchalariga mos keladi.

She is cooking dinner now.U hozir kechki ovqat tayyorlayapti.

4. Qachon ishlatiladi?

① Hozir bo'layotgan harakat

I am reading a book now.Men hozir kitob o'qiyapman.

② Ko'z o'ngimizda (Look! Listen!)

Look! It is raining.Qara! Yomg'ir yog'yapti.

③ O'zgarayotgan vaziyat

The population is growing.Aholi soni o'syapti.

④ Aniq kelajak rejasi

I am meeting Ali tomorrow.Ertaga Ali bilan uchrashaman (kelishilgan).

⑤ Vaqtinchalik holat

I am living with my aunt this month.Shu oy xolamnikida turyapman.

⑥ always bilan — g'ashlik

6. Continuous'da ishlatilmaydigan fe'llar (State verbs)

Ba'zi fe'llar harakatni emas, holat, his-tuyg'u yoki fikrni bildiradi. Ular odatda Present Continuous'da ishlatilmaydi.

GuruhFe'llar
His-tuyg'ulike, love, hate, want, need, prefer
Fikrknow, understand, believe, think (= fikrda bo'lmoq), remember, forget, mean
Sezgisee, hear, smell, taste, seem, sound
Egalikhave (= ega bo'lmoq), own, belong, contain
✗ I am knowing the answer.✓ I know the answer.Men javobni bilaman.
